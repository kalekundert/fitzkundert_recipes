Dutch Baby
==========

Ingredients
-----------
Dry:
- 67 g (½ cup) flour (sifted)
- 38 g (3 tbsp) sugar
- ½ tsp kosher salt

Wet:
- 2 large eggs
- 120 mL whole milk
- 14 g (1 tbsp) butter (melted)
- 1 tsp (5 g) vanilla extract or 1/2 tsp lemon extract

Miscellaneous:
- 2 tbsp butter
- Lemon wedges
- Powdered sugar

Directions
----------
1. Preheat oven to 375°F.

2. Put a cast iron skillet in the preheating oven, with 3 tbsp butter.

3. Mix the dry ingredients.

   - I like to sift the flour, because it makes it easier to get a smooth 
     batter with no lumps, but it isn't necessary.

4. Mix the wet ingredients in the following order [1]:

   - Eggs (beaten)
   - Milk
   - Butter (melted, from the preheating skillet)

   The order does seem to matter!  I don't know why, but I tried mixing these 
   ingredients in a couple different ways, and this gives the best rise.

5. Wait until the butter in the skillet turns a golden brown.

   - Often you can start to smell a sweet, nutty aroma when this happens.

6. Mix the wet and dry ingredients to form a smooth, thin batter.

7. Pour the batter into the preheated skillet.

5. Bake for 30-35 minutes; until the edges are puffed and brown.

6. Sprinkle with powdered sugar and serve with lemon wedges.

   - beat eggs, add milk, add melted butter
