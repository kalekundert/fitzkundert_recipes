Berry Fruit Snacks
==================

Ingredients
-----------
- 1½ cups frozen berries, defrosted and drained
- 1½ cups plain Greek yogurt
- 2 cups 100% fruit juice
- 1 Tbsp lime juice
- ¼ cup gelatin

Directions
----------
- Make sure the berries are fully thawed and drained without any remaining ice 
  crystals.

- Let the berries and yogurt both come to room temperature. This helps to 
  create a smooth consistency instead of a lumpy mixture.

- Blend the berries and yogurt until completely smooth, creamy, and evenly 
  colored. Set aside for now.

- Pour half of the fruit juice (1 cup) into a medium-sized stainless-steel bowl 
  or glass baking dish. Sprinkle the gelatin slowly and evenly across the 
  surface. Let it sit for 5 to 10 minutes so it can absorb the liquid and swell 
  properly.

- Pour the remaining fruit juice into a small saucepan and bring it to a 
  simmer. You want it to be hot and gently steaming, not rolling or 
  aggressively boiling.

- Once the juice is warm, take the saucepan off the heat and stir in the lime 
  juice.

- Pour the warmed juice into the gelatin mixture and whisk until it is silky 
  and fully dissolved. The liquid should look glossy and smooth. Let it cool.

- Use the food thermometer to make sure your gelatin mixture gets below 110°F
  before continuing.

- Once at the correct temperature, pour the gelatin mixture into the blender 
  with the fruit/yogurt puree. Blend at a low speed until everything is just 
  combined. Don’t overdo it! This helps to limit the amount of air that gets 
  mixed in.

- Pour the finished product into your silicone molds. Transfer to the 
  refrigerator and chill until fully set. Serve cold for the best chewy, sweet 
  bite!
