# Rich Hot Chocolate

## Ingredients

- 1 cup (236 mL) milk
- 32 g unsweetened baking chocolate
- 32 g sugar
- vanilla

## Directions

- Combine milk, cocoa, and sugar in a small sauce pot.

- Heat until milk begins to steam and foam a bit.

  - Use medium low heat, to avoid scalding the milk.
  - Stir with a whisk, to get the cocoa to dissolve.

- Add a splash of vanilla after removing from heat.

## Notes

- This recipe is a good way to use up extra baking cocoa left over from making 
  chocolate cake.
